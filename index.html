About our company

indogreen export is an Indonesian export company committed to delivering premium banana leaves to the global market. We proudly partner with local farmers, empowering rural communities and promoting sustainable agriculture.

Our banana leaves are freshly harvested, naturally grown, and carefully processed to meet international food-grade standards. We serve eco-conscious businesses in the food industry, catering services, and biodegradable packaging sectors.
