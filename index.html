About our company

Indogreen Export is an Indonesian export company committed to delivering premium turmeric and banana leaf to the global market. We proudly partner with local farmers, empowering rural communities and promoting sustainable agriculture.

Our products are freshly harvested, naturally grown, and carefully processed to meet international food-grade standards. We serve health-conscious businesses in the food industry, herbal medicine, natural cosmetics, and traditional packaging sectors.
