Banana Leaf Product Types

We provide various types of high quality banana leaves according to your needs

Banana leaves

Cleaned, tidied, and folded neatly. Vacuum packed to maintain freshness. Ready for export. Premium quality.

  • Packinging Size: 30-40 cm
  • Packaging: Vacuum pack
  • Shelf life: 15-30 days in cold temperature (chiller 5-10°C)

Round Banana Leaves

Cleaned and cut into circular shape. Vacuum packed to extend shelf life. Ideal for export. Premium quality.

  • Diameter: 30–40 cm
  • Vacuum pack (50 sheets per pack)
  • 15–30 days in cold temperature (chiller 5–10°C)

Dried Banana Leaves

Naturally dried banana leaves. Suitable for crafts and decoration.

  • Size: 40–60 cm (can be customized)
  • Packaging: Bundled or folded, 50 sheets per pack
  • Shelf life: 6–12 months in dry storage
