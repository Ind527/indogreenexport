About Us

Get to know more about the trusted premium banana leaf export company

Company History

Indogreen Export was founded in 2025 with a clear vision: to become Indonesia’s leading exporter of high-quality banana leaves. Although newly established, our company is built on strong values of sustainability, innovation, and collaboration with trusted local farmers in Banten.

As global demand increases for natural and eco-friendly products, Indogreen Export is preparing to enter the international market with a commitment to deliver premium-grade banana leaves. With advanced post-harvest handling methods and a focus on quality control, we aim to meet the expectations of global culinary, hospitality, and food service industries.

We are in the early stages of building strategic partnerships and export readiness to soon serve clients across Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Europe.

Visi Kami

To be the leading banana leaf exporter in Southeast Asia, known for its premium quality, sustainable innovation, and commitment to environmental conservation and empowerment of local farming communities.

Our Mission

  • Providing high quality banana leaves that meet international standards
  • Building long-term partnerships with local farmers
  • Implementing sustainable and environmentally friendly business practices
  • Providing the best service to all customers
  • Contributing to the local and national economy
